---
name: Catalog artwork provenance
description: Why cross-catalog image matching must tolerate unavailable artwork.
---

Treat unavailable catalog artwork as a valid result, not a reason to substitute a same-name printing.

**Why:** Real scanner suggestions have combined anniversary Exeggcute identities with Pokémon Pocket images and Mega Evolution Energy identities with Obsidian Flames images. Name and collector number alone do not establish a printing across catalogs. Artwork availability does not establish price provenance either.

**How to apply:** When bridging catalogs, require independently verified set and collector identity and explicit upstream artwork. Keep the original selectable identity and its pricing separate from image enrichment. Do not invent missing printings to make a suggestion look complete; use unavailable artwork or the captured scan.
