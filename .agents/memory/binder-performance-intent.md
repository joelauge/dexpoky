---
name: Binder performance intent
description: The user's performance and existing-image requirements for the binder feature.
---

Binder work must be "highly performant and use the images we're already loading for all the card sets."

**Why:** The user explicitly made performance and reuse of existing set artwork requirements for adding cards, holder pages, drag-and-drop, page turns, page sizes, and different binders.

**How to apply:** Preserve those requirements when extending binders. Do not introduce full-catalog image preloading or duplicate artwork acquisition just to populate the binder.

The 3D view should have more "flexible" pages and allow the user to "somewhat move around the 3D space" or have it slightly respond to mouse or device movement.

Pages should visibly "curl" when turned.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these qualities for the binder viewer and repeated the flexibility request as curling during page turns.

**How to apply:** Keep page motion visibly flexible during turns, not just gently bowed at rest, and spatial movement restrained while preserving the performance and existing-artwork requirements above.

Remove ring/zip/folio selectors and their differences in the 3D window. Focus on flexible pages and look/feel "as good as a triple-A game studio."

**Why:** The user explicitly replaced the earlier binder-style direction with this requirement.

**How to apply:** Keep saved layouts compatible, but do not reintroduce style-specific controls or hardware as visual options. Prioritize page motion and tactile presentation.

"The previous and next pages in the binder should be pre-composed so the pages don't refresh with the cards when they come into view."

**Why:** The user explicitly requested ready adjacent pages rather than cards refreshing during page turns.

**How to apply:** Preserve adjacent page compositions and existing artwork across turns, while keeping preparation bounded rather than preloading an entire binder.
