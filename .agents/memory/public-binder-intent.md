---
name: Public binder intent
description: The collector's requirement for sharing binders and receiving offers on wanted cards.
---

The user wants to make a binder public for others to view and turn any empty binder pocket into a wishlist card so visitors can contact the owner about giving, selling, or trading that card.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a public binder as a way to show both their collection and the cards they want.

**How to apply:** Keep wishlist placeholders distinct from owned copies and preserve the visitor-to-owner contact path when changing binder presentation or collection workflows.

Public sharing should start from a Share button on the binder page and open a full-browser-page 3D binder with a flat-view toggle, page turning, and animated card pull-out and put-back. Visitors should see additional card images and make buy or trade offers on owned cards.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested an interactive shared binder, not only a static public collection.

For a wishlist card, a visitor who owns the desired card should be able to sell their copy to the owner. Visitors without an account must be told that a DexPoky account is required to offer cards for sale to other users.

**Why:** The user wants the existing Share/Sell process to take over for signed-in sellers, with the negotiation in both users' Inboxes.

**How to apply:** Preserve that Share/Sell-to-Inbox handoff rather than inventing a separate binder sale process.
